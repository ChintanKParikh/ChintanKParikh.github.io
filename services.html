How We Work

What working with us looks like

1

Getting to Know You

We meet over coffee or chai and talk about how you live, what you need and what you want to spend. The more we understand, the better the design.

2

First Ideas

We prepare sketches, AutoCAD drawings, 3D views, walkthroughs and material samples, so you can see the idea before anything is built.

3

Detailing

We work through every measurement, material and switch position with detailed drawings and realistic 3D renders, until you are happy with it.

4

On Site

Our team and craftsmen build it, and we keep you updated as the work moves along.

5

Welcome Home

We hand over the finished space with final styling, complete documentation and support after you move in.
