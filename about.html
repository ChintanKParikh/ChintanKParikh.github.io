The KCP Interia Story

Who we are and how we work

Every home is different because every family is different. We start by understanding how you live, what you need from each room and how you want it to feel. Then we design around that.

Since 2008 we have completed more than 300 projects in Hyderabad and beyond: family homes, villas, hotels, offices, showrooms and shops. Each one was planned and built together with the people who would use it.

What we do best is listen. Good interiors come from understanding the people who will live or work in them, not from following trends.

Meet Chintan K Parikh

Founder and principal designer

CP

Founder & Principal Interior Designer

Chintan K Parikh started KCP Interia in 2008 and has led the design of every project since. The idea was simple: make spaces that people enjoy living and working in.

He pays close attention to detail, from the choice of materials to the finish in every corner, and keeps clients involved in every decision. A space should be practical for daily life and still feel special to come home to.

None of this happens alone. KCP Interia is a close team of designers and craftsmen who work side by side, look out for one another, and care about every home as if it were their own. Every project is planned, built and finished together, and together we have completed more than 300 projects, including family homes, plug and play corporate offices, jewellery showrooms and commercial spaces.
